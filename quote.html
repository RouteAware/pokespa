Common questions

Can you fix my card now?

Not right now. These guides cover what you can safely do at home:

  • White edges or corners: Edge and corner whitening
  • Creases and bends: Creases, bends and pressing
  • Grime, residue, fingerprints: How to clean Pokémon cards safely and Wax, gum and stains on vintage
  • Scratches on a holo: Holo scratches
  • Dents and dings: Indents, dings and lifted edges

Will restoration raise my card's grade?

No. PSA, BGS and CGC count pressing, repairs and cleaning solutions as alteration. See Cleaning cards before grading.

How do I protect the card until then?

Penny sleeve, then a toploader, stored flat and dry. The card-care shop has what we use.
