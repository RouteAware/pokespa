Privacy

What this site collects, and how to say no.

Plain words, no tricks. Effective September 6, 2026.

The short version. PokéSpa is a Recursis Holdings venture. We collect what you send through the quote form (including your card photos), the email you give us for the drops digest, cookieless page-view counts, and — unless you turn it off below — a Meta ad pixel. Some shop links are Amazon affiliate links. We don't sell your data, we don't trade it with data brokers, and a person answers info@pokespa.com.

What we collect and why

The quote form. Requesting a quote asks for your name, email, a description of the card, and up to four photos. Photos are shrunk in your browser before they're sent. The whole thing is turned into one email — photos attached — and sent to our inbox through Resend, our email provider, with your address set as reply-to so we can answer you. We use it to assess the card and reply, and for nothing else. Your photos are never posted anywhere.

The drops digest. If you sign up on the Drops page, your email goes into a mailing list held by Resend and you get a short weekly email about upcoming sets. Every email has an unsubscribe link; one click removes you. We don't add anyone to that list who didn't sign up for it, including people who send a quote request.

Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count page views and measure how fast pages load. They are cookieless, don't build a profile of you, and don't follow you across other sites. We see aggregate numbers — pages, countries, device types, load times — not people. How Vercel handles it.

The ad pixel. This site loads the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) so we can tell whether our ads bring people here, and show our ads to people who have visited. The pixel sets cookies in your browser and sends Meta the pages you view here, with your browser and IP details. For visitors in California we send Meta the "Limited Data Use" flag, which tells Meta to process that data under California's restrictions. Under California's definition this counts as "sharing" data for advertising, so we give you a real switch: the button below stops the pixel from loading on this browser, and we also honor your browser's Global Privacy Control signal automatically. Meta's privacy policy covers what Meta does with what it receives.

Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, PokéSpa earns from qualifying purchases. Links marked "(paid link)" on the Shop page carry our affiliate tag. When you click one, Amazon sets its own cookies and handles everything from there under Amazon's privacy notice. Amazon tells us what was bought through our links in aggregate; it does not tell us who bought it.

The merch shop. shop.pokespa.com is hosted by Fourthwall, which is the merchant of record: Fourthwall takes your payment, prints, ships, and handles returns under its own privacy policy and terms (linked in the shop footer). We can see order details in the shop dashboard so we can help with a problem; we don't get your card number.

The tools. The value lookup sends your search terms from your browser to the community Pokémon TCG API to fetch results; the show finder links you out to Google Maps and Facebook searches, which run under their own policies. The Drops page pulls set dates and headlines from public sources on our server; nothing about you goes with those requests.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data, and we never hand it to data brokers.
  • We don't combine what you send in a form with the ad pixel or analytics.
  • We don't send marketing email to quote requesters. The only list is the drops digest, and only for people who signed up for it.
  • This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Turn the ad pixel off with the button below, or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser.
  • Leave the drops list with the unsubscribe link in any digest email.
  • See, correct or delete anything you've sent us — photos included: email info@pokespa.com from the address you used and we'll do it, usually within a few days.
  • Block cookies in your browser if you'd rather — the site works fine without them.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
