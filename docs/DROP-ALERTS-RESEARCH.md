# Pokémon Center drop notifications — research & verdict (2026-09-01)

Alexander's idea: paid Discords sell Pokémon Center restock/drop alerts —
could PokéSpa offer drop notifications?

## The market
Commoditized at $0–$10/mo: Poke Alerts $5.99, PokePings $8.99, PokeNotify
$7.99, PokeFreeze $7.99, Plugged Inn $6.99→$34.99 VIP (auto-checkout tier),
House of Carts (cook group). FREE and nearly as good: TrackaLacker (40k
members), TYPA, Restockd app, HotStock, @PokeAlerts_ on X, r/PKMNTCGDeals,
DIY Visualping (2,371 active monitors on pokemoncenter.com pages). The paid
premium is speed + auto-checkout — the toxic part.

## Why we must NOT run our own monitor
1. **Explicit TOS ban**: pokemoncenter.com Terms of Use prohibits "data
   mining, robots or similar data gathering or extraction methods" and apps
   that interact with the service without written consent. Parent TPCi terms
   ban bots/scripts outright.
2. **Hard anti-bot stack**: DataDome + Queue-it virtual waiting room (added
   Feb 2025) + hCaptcha. It's a perpetual arms race.
3. **Enforcement is real**: Sept 2025 Phantasmal Flames — TPCi mass-canceled
   ~42,000 botted preorders.
4. **The brand equation**: PokéSpa's whole posture is staying clean with
   TPCi (unregistered Poké- name, nominative use only). Breaching their TOS
   with a scraper is the one move that invites the C&D we've engineered
   around. CFAA criminal risk is low post-Van Buren/hiQ, but hiQ LOST on
   breach-of-contract — civil TOS claims are live.
5. Pending Stopping Grinch Bots Act would make circumventing purchase
   controls an FTC violation.

## Recommended v1 (zero-scrape, on-brand) — awaiting Alexander's go
1. **/drops.html — Pokémon Drop Calendar**: editorially maintained release +
   preorder dates from public announcements (TPCi news, PokeBeach, retailer
   listings). SEO cluster: "pokemon center restock schedule".
2. **"How to win drop day" guide**: how the Queue-it waiting room actually
   works (one tab, don't refresh), account/payment prep, why bots get
   canceled (Phantasmal Flames story) — perfect honest-brand content, and
   funnels to slab care ("got the box? protect the hits").
3. **Curated FREE alert-tool directory**: TYPA, Restockd, TrackaLacker,
   HotStock, @PokeAlerts_, r/PKMNTCGDeals — generous link-outs that undercut
   the paid Discords' pitch. Zero legal exposure.
4. **Weekly email digest** ("drops this week") — human-curated, via Resend;
   starts the PokéSpa list. Later option: community-reported restock pings.

Rules if built: never automate against pokemoncenter.com; "Pokémon Center"
referentially only + existing disclaimer; never promise real-time speed;
never embed/resell paid groups' feeds.
