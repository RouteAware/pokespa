## What was not done, and why

- **No Product/Offer schema.** The shop has price ranges, not prices. Marking up an `Offer` with a
  range we do not control would be a claim the site cannot back.
- **No body copy rewritten.** Only `<head>` metadata, structured data and link targets.
  `git diff` on the copy commit shows zero body lines changed.
- **No design restructuring.** The heading fix changes `<p class="cat">` to `<h2 class="cat">` and pins
  the font weight so nothing moves; the link rebalance swaps one `<li>` for another.
- **WebP/AVIF not adopted.** It would beat JPEG by another ~30%, but it needs `<picture>` elements
  on every image — that is a design change and a separate job.
- **`X-Robots-Tag` and caching headers untouched.** `vercel.json` already does the right thing.
