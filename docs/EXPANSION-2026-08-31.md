# PokéSpa expansion plan — Service + Shop + Learn
_Research date: 2026-08-31 (three-agent deep dive: market/SEO, products/vendors,
legal/positioning). This doc is the truth for the expansion until superseded._

## The thesis (Alexander's, validated)
Hands-on restoration stays minimal/on-demand. The growth engine is:
1. **Learn** — an education hub on damage types, remedies, and tools (SEO owns it)
2. **Shop** — a curated store of card-care/restoration products (no inventory)
3. **Service** — the existing quote funnel, fed by both ("DIY this, send us that")

Research verdict: **the space is wide open.** No authority site exists; content
is fragmented across YouTube/TikTok, Shopify blogs, and forum folklore. The
closest model competitor is Poke Master Center (store + tutorials + podcast).
There is real, autocomplete-confirmed demand for "pokemon card cleaning service
/ near me" that product-only competitors can't serve — PokéSpa can.

## Positioning — the line that protects us (from legal research)
**"Conservation, not doctoring."** Mirror NGC/PCGS coin conservation and comic
pressing (CGC's own affiliate presses comics) — restoration-with-disclosure is
an established, legitimized category. The crime in every real case (Mastro's
trimmed Wagner card → mail fraud) was *undisclosed* alteration at sale.

**Hard lines (additions to CLAUDE.md hard rules):**
1. **NEVER grade-improvement marketing.** PSA treats cleaning solutions, gloss,
   scratch removal, even crease pressing as alteration (N5/N7) and has
   retroactively decertified Kurt's-treated cards. Copy frames products as
   *card care / display prep / preservation* — never "raise your grade."
   Education says plainly: per current grader policy, a restored card is an
   altered card; restore for display/play/preservation, not to chase grades.
2. **Publish the charter**: what we will never do or teach for deception — no
   trimming, no recoloring, no filling, no concealment; always disclose when
   selling/grading. (Extends the existing PokéSpa promise; it's also our E-E-A-T
   moat — community red lines: reversible + nothing added/removed = acceptable.)
3. **FTC affiliate disclosure** (16 CFR 255, 2023): "paid link"-style disclosure
   adjacent to EVERY monetized recommendation, visible with it — footer
   boilerplate is not compliant.
4. **Trademark care, tightened**: nominative use only ("for Pokémon cards"),
   keep the non-affiliation footer, no Pokémon imagery ever. **Never file a
   USPTO application for "PokéSpa"** — TPCi actively opposes Poké-prefix marks
   in the card space (Poké Go 2017; 2025 opposition vs a hobby shop's
   Pikachu-*reminiscent* logo). The name is our biggest standing trademark
   exposure; the store raises visibility. Accepted risk, monitor, keep clean.
5. **Product liability**: sell reputable brands unmodified under their own
   labels (relabeling = "apparent manufacturer" liability); no safety/efficacy
   claims beyond the manufacturer's. Sales tax: home state first; platform
   automation when own checkout exists; monitor Wayfair thresholds (~$100k/state).

## Learn — content architecture (16 pages, from SEO research)
Biggest query cluster fuses cleaning with grading anxiety ("…before grading",
"…for psa"). Winning content answers "how" AND "will it flag as altered."

**Pillar + damage encyclopedia:** (1) How to Clean Pokémon Cards Safely [pillar]
· (2) Cleaning Before Grading: what PSA/BGS/CGC actually flag · (3) Damage
Encyclopedia hub → (4) Holo scratches · (5) Edge/corner whitening · (6) Creases
& pressing · (7) Wax/gum/stains on vintage · (8) Warping & humidity de-warping
(the one remedy collectors bless) · (9) Indents & lifted edges · (10) Cleaning
vs Card Doctoring: where the line is [trust anchor].
**Commercial:** (11) Best cleaning kits compared (Kurt's vs TCG Card Care vs
Giovanni's vs generic) · (12) Kurt's Card Care review · (13) Supplies you
actually need · (14) Shop category pages.
**Service funnel:** (15) Professional cleaning/restoration service page
(targets "service near me / cost" — our unique rankable claim) · (16) DIY or
Pro? when to send it in [the converter].
Later: 30–60s TikTok/YouTube versions per damage type — discovery lives there
and no channel owns it (study: Reshoevn8r's education→product model).

jobs, short-form video channel.
