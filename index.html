How it works

01

Send photos

Email a few shots of the card and tell us what's wrong — creases, grime, edges, whatever it is.

02

Honest assessment

We tell you what can improve, what can't, and what we'd do — before anything is agreed.

03

Careful restoration

04

Full documentation

Before-and-after photos and a plain list of exactly what was done, kept with the card.

The PokéSpa promise

Restoration, disclosed. Always.

You'll know everything we did

Every card leaves with its documentation. No mystery work, ever.

We'll say no when we should

Some damage is permanent. If restoration won't help — or could hurt — we tell you before we start.

Disclose when you sell or grade

A restored card is a restored card. We never help pass one off as untouched, and neither should you.
