What we'll never do

  • No trimming, recoloring, or filling. That's not restoration — it's card doctoring, and it's where hobby fraud cases come from.
  • No grade promises. Under current PSA, BGS, CGC and TAG policy, restoration work counts as alteration. A restored card is an altered card. Restoration is for display, play, and preservation, never to chase a grade. Restoration orders are closed; join the waitlist. Read exactly what graders flag.
  • No mystery work. Every card leaves with its documentation, and every restored card should be disclosed as restored when sold or graded. Where we draw the line.
