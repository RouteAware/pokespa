The service

Careful hands for tired cards.

A restoration service that tells you the truth first. Free photo assessment, conservative work, and full documentation of everything we did — because restoration is disclosed, always.

What we take on

Cleaning

Surface grime, residue, and stains lifted without harming the card.

De-creasing & flattening

Bends, creases, and warping eased back toward flat.

Edge & corner work

Frayed edges and soft corners cleaned up and reinforced.

Surface repair

Scratches, print lines, and blemishes carefully addressed.

Pokémon cards are our home turf, but collectible trading cards generally — other TCGs, sports cards — are welcome. The older and more delicate the card, the more conservative we get.

What about cost?

Every card is different — different damage, different stock, different risk — so every card is quoted individually after we see photos. The assessment costs nothing, and you'll have the quote in hand before any work begins. If a card isn't worth restoring, we'll tell you that too.

What we'll never do

  • No trimming, recoloring, or filling. That's not restoration — it's card doctoring, and it's where hobby fraud cases come from.
  • No grade promises. Under current PSA, BGS, CGC and TAG policy, restoration work counts as alteration. A restored card is an altered card — we restore for display, play, and preservation, never to chase a grade. Read exactly what graders flag.
  • No mystery work. Every card leaves with its documentation, and every restored card should be disclosed as restored when sold or graded. Where we draw the line.
