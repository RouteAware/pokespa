Before you buy anything for a card you might grade:

PSA, BGS, CGC and TAG treat cleaning solutions, polishes, and added gloss as alteration. If a card is headed to a grader, the only "gear" it needs is an air blower, a dry microfiber cloth, and a sleeve. Read what graders actually flag →

Bench tools — flattening, honestly

Pressing is alteration — full stop.

Every grader treats pressed-out creases and wrinkles as an altered card. These tools are for warped or curled display and play copies you want to sit flat — never for prepping a card to grade or sell as untouched. Where the line is →

The gentle method

Acrylic plates + soft clamps

Two small acrylic sheets and soft-jaw spring clamps, light pressure, patience measured in days — the conservative way to coax a warped card flat.
