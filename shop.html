Slab care — for graded cards

The one place polish is honest:

A graded slab is a plastic case — polishing scuffs out of the case is fair game and touches nothing the grader graded. The rule stays simple: polish the case, never the card. And keep slabs out of direct sun — the case blocks fingers, not UV.
