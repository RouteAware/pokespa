How to actually win drop day

01

One tab. Don't refresh.

Pokémon Center uses a randomized virtual waiting room. Opening ten tabs or refreshing doesn't move you up — it can reset you. One tab, one wait.

02

Prep the account the night before

Signed in, address saved, payment saved. Drop-day carts die during typing. The fastest checkout is the one with nothing left to fill in.

03

Set alerts where they're free

The free tools below cover the same monitors the paid Discords sell. Turn on notifications for the one or two you'll actually see.

04

Don't buy from bots — and don't be one

When bots bypassed the queue on a recent preorder, The Pokémon Company canceled roughly 42,000 orders. Play it straight; the reprint always comes.
