The big recurring shows worth traveling for

The one everyone means

The National (NSCC)

The National Sports Collectors Convention — the hobby's biggest annual show, rotating between a handful of host cities each summer. Sports-first, but the TCG floor grows every year.

TCG-first circuit

Collect-A-Con

The traveling trading-card-and-anime convention — multiple cities across the year, heavy Pokémon presence, big trade-night energy.

If you're near Los Angeles

Frank & Son Collectible Show

The legendary City of Industry warehouse show — running twice a week for decades. A permanent card show, effectively.

East coast staples

White Plains & Chantilly

The White Plains (NY) show and the Chantilly (VA) show are the long-running monthly-ish anchors of the northeast circuit.

Card-shop nights

Trade nights at your LCS

Most local card shops run weekly Pokémon trade nights — the easiest rep to build, and where you'll hear about every regional show first.

Always verify

Check dates before you drive

Show schedules move. Confirm dates on the organizer's own page or socials before traveling — the local searches above surface the current listings.
